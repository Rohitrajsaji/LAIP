# Private validation boundary

Some local regression inputs and their derived facts, reports, screenshots and oracle assertions have unconfirmed redistribution rights. They are retained privately in the development workspace and withheld from the source candidate. Original banking files have never been included in the application release.

The public candidate retains synthetic contract, persistence, consumer and snapshot-version tests. It excludes source-dependent private regressions coherently; their absence is not a private acceptance pass. The public synthetic workflow is independently exercised through import, analysis, evidence, review, retrieval and export.

Source-excluded exports can still contain attributed evidence snippets. Review their content and authorization before supplying them to a downstream AI system. Private or live-system results must not be inferred from synthetic test success.
